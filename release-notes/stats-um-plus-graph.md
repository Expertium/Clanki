- The Stats page in Advanced mode has a new UM+ cross-comparison. Pick a pair of
  algorithms and it shows, for each group of reviews the two disagreed about by
  about the same amount, how far each of them was from what really happened. A
  line that stays near zero across the whole width is the better algorithm: the
  legend gives each one its UM+ score, where closer to zero is better, and the
  slope of its line. The size of a bubble is the number of reviews in that group,
  and a switch adds the groups of fewer than 200 reviews, which are hidden by
  default.
- The groups and the weighting are the ones the FSRS benchmark uses, so the
  numbers can be compared with the published ones.
