Simple mode no longer uses the word "retrievability". It says "Probability of
recall" instead: the Browser's column, the tooltip of the forgetting curve in
card info, the "Cards selected by" orders of the filtered-deck dialog, and the
message that appears when RWKV cannot prepare the scores of a filtered deck.
Advanced mode keeps the technical word. Only the text changes; the search
syntax (`prop:r`), the order of the cards and the numbers are the same.
