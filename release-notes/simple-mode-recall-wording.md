- One setting decides whether Clanki says "retrievability" or "probability of
  recall": **Replace "retrievability" with "probability of recall"**, in
  Preferences > UI split, with three choices — **In Simple mode only** (the
  default), **Never: always say "retrievability"**, and **Always, in both
  modes**. It reaches every text that names the chance of recalling a card:
  the Browser's column, card info and the tooltip of its forgetting curve,
  the "Cards selected by" orders of the filtered-deck dialog, the message
  that appears when RWKV cannot prepare the scores of a filtered deck, the
  deck-options orders and help text (New card gather order, Review sort
  order, the RWKV-Instant recommendation, Minimum reviews per day), the
  Advance and Postpone dialogs, and the Stats graphs with their tooltips and
  tables (Card Probability of Recall, the Stability subtitle, the Total
  Knowledge subtitle). Only the text changes: the search syntax (`prop:r`),
  the order of the cards and the numbers are the same. A screen reads the
  setting while it builds its names, so reopen a window (the Browser, deck
  options, card info, the Stats page) that was already open when you changed
  it.
