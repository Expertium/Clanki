- The note editor now has the same "Simple | Advanced" switch as the main
  window, deck options and the statistics, at the right end of its toolbar.
  Simple mode keeps Fields..., Bold, Italic, Underline, the text colour,
  Remove formatting, Attach pictures/audio/video and the Tags row. Advanced
  mode also shows Cards..., the editor's settings gear, Superscript,
  Subscript, the text highlight colour, the two list buttons, Alignment,
  Record audio and Equations. Buttons that add-ons add show in both modes, and
  every hidden button keeps its keyboard shortcut.
