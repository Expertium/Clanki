- The Browser follows the Simple | Advanced switch. Simple mode shows the
  items most people use: search, the sidebar's Today, Flags, Card State, Decks
  and Tags, a fixed set of columns (Sort Field, Deck, Due, Interval), and the
  menu items to add, tag (every tag item), suspend, flag, move or delete cards. Advanced mode
  shows everything as before, with your own columns. Hidden menu items keep
  their keyboard shortcuts.
