- Burying is now one setting, "Hide related cards until tomorrow", with the
  same name in Simple and Advanced mode. The words "related cards" are
  underlined and explain themselves when you rest the pointer on them: "cards
  that belong to the same note". The three separate switches ("Bury new
  siblings", "Bury review siblings", "Bury interday learning siblings") are
  gone, so burying is on or off for a preset rather than per card type. A
  preset that arrives from another client with only some of them on shows
  "Partly on" until you toggle the switch.
